## 🔎 Defining Scope

### Definition
> [!INFO] What is Scoping?
>
> **Scoping** defines the boundaries within which security activities will be conducted. It includes specifying target systems, exclusion criteria, timelines, and resource limitations.

### Importance of Proper Scoping
> [!NOTE]
> Well-defined scope ensures that assessments are both effective and compliant with legal requirements.
